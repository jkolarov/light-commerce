# Visual references: default theme

Source: Mobbin, searched 2026-11-03. Collection: [light-commerce: theme references](https://mobbin.com/collections/4cd33b2c-ad75-418a-a118-0b9533c540e3). It holds 23 sections, 5 screens and 1 flow, and it is private: only the owner and teammates can open it.

**Gaps.** Web captures are desktop; narrower ones include Escape Cafe's product list and the Savor and Kalstore product pages, none of them true mobile layouts. Header/language-switch and card-form/confirmation searches returned mostly SaaS pages, so that evidence below is thinner.

## Directions

### A. Editorial quiet
*Magazine-like: large, quiet headings (serif in the refs), an off-white page, thin rules and one dark button style.*
Refs: Assembly Coffee [hero](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/747e1e51-77c1-4dd5-a556-a67199054387), [coffee grid](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/a4f80396-d25b-470b-a707-f6a0e25283e5), [story](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/73e58c34-7960-457e-990f-9e43ae4bf460); Savor [product page](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/07791cdc-c5e6-4a6d-b45d-66c89508afb6).
Why it survives a swap: type scale and whitespace set the hierarchy, not colour. Primary appears only on buttons and links, so any palette stays calm.

### B. Warm producer (origin first)
*Tinted background, product shots on matching tiles, origin and process facts, small labels, dotted rules.*
Refs: Graza [hero](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/64e41fb0-c4fa-4f69-a03a-707d52b26b9c), [shop grid](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/f86d7f5d-fd2c-4cc5-b199-0e86d955d470), [origin story](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/2eff73a5-3e21-4a2c-a7b4-fe9100a464b9), [footer](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/e0b0a006-0495-4969-ab1a-5b35acc33e8d); Little Amps Coffee [grid](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/9c4d05e3-7ce2-4f2f-b5ac-bf417bcc2e7e), [product page](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/8ebb5ee0-3959-49fb-ac05-1fecdb3a51b6); Escape Cafe [all products](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/c2bdfec6-82d4-4e22-baaa-5f866e5c7cdf).
Why it survives a swap: each part maps to one token (background, surface tiles, primary for CTAs, muted for labels). The character comes from content every producer has: place, people, process.

### C. Bold colour-block
*Primary fills whole sections, with chunky display type, pill buttons and illustration.*
Refs: MANA Yerba Maté [header and flavours](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/51751d10-1256-4319-bc6e-4935e0fb2a03), [product page](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/397dea4b-1996-40fc-9527-88406c00385b); re_ [product page](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/b3bbfa7a-e557-47ec-b9dd-10c6bb6f3008); Monte [contact](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/6ecba422-fe26-482b-8225-8e24ec959d84).
Why it only partly survives: it needs a strong, saturated primary. A pale or muted one looks washed out or garish.

## Recommendation
B's structure with A's restraint. Small producers sell provenance, and B has slots for it (origin facts, people, place). A's discipline (one accent, type-led hierarchy) stops a swapped palette looking like a template. Offer C only as an opt-in "primary-tone section" block.

## Patterns per screen

**Home**
- Large headline or wordmark with a one-line promise, then products straight away ([Graza hero](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/64e41fb0-c4fa-4f69-a03a-707d52b26b9c)).
- Announcement bar that states a real fact, such as a free-shipping threshold ([Assembly hero](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/747e1e51-77c1-4dd5-a556-a67199054387)).
- Origin block with a photo and a place label ([Graza origin story](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/2eff73a5-3e21-4a2c-a7b4-fe9100a464b9)).

**Product list**
- The same `surface` tile behind every photo, so mismatched photography still looks intentional (Escape Cafe, Graza grid).
- Each card shows the name in the heading font, one line of descriptors (tasting notes) and a "From — €X" button (Assembly grid).
- With 4–6 products, skip the filter sidebar. At most use category tabs (Graza grid).

**Product page**
- Gallery with thumbnails; title, price, short copy, stepper and a full-width add-to-cart that shows the price (Little Amps, re_).
- A key/value fact column (Origin / Producer / Process), configurable for vintage, varietal or flower source (Little Amps).
- Accordions for ingredients and for shipping and delivery (Savor, re_).

**Cart**
- A right-hand drawer that opens on add: thumbnail and stepper per line, a total, one primary CTA, and a "View cart" link to a full page ([Uvodo drawer](https://mobbin.com/screens/10f9cdb6-ee40-46d0-9d7a-02c420ed1b92)).
- Totals rows with hairline separators and a CTA in the brand colour ([Hims drawer](https://mobbin.com/screens/3d578447-7e0c-47e5-b1bd-6820e5f7edb3)).

**Checkout**
- Logo only, no site nav. Form left; summary right with thumbnails, discount field, totals; policy links in the footer ([Shop flow, Bones Coffee](https://mobbin.com/flows/a0e48128-6481-47ca-9f84-8cfac24fabaf)).
- Few, calm sections (contact, payment), product image in the summary ([Flodesk checkout](https://mobbin.com/screens/5287e7ec-4ef2-45b0-9d55-097c55ba0cd7)).

**Confirmation**
- "Thank you, {name}" with the order number, a details grid (contact, shipping, payment) and the summary column ([Shop confirmation](https://mobbin.com/screens/8aa92818-60d0-4aed-a40b-8829771cbd55)).
- A warm headline in the heading font, plus a line pointing to the seller if something is wrong ([Etsy confirmation](https://mobbin.com/screens/5a4d57ea-e2e9-48f0-934d-852f56562c51)).

**Content pages**
- Large serif pull-quote with a sign-off and one real photo ([Graza story](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/c0551959-279d-4c6f-ad77-0998844fd5c3)). Founders' sign-off with a photo ([Yellowbird](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/19c349e1-ff13-40ce-96af-26ea0ccb4248)).
- An opening sentence that says who, where and since when (Assembly story).
- Contact: people photo, headline, email links ([Little Amps contact](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/7779ce52-eea3-4981-8d82-b097f9ede54b)); location card with address, hours, map link ([Little Amps location](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/74a3a0da-d93a-419b-a675-1a641b46a4eb)); or Contact / Hours / Find us columns (Monte).

**Header and footer**
- Header: logo, 3–4 text links, a text "FR" toggle and the cart (MANA's "Fr" pill; [Escape Cafe](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/46dccfc3-ac20-47f0-aef3-df77321fff3d) puts "FR" in a side rail).
- Footer: newsletter, three link columns, an address and opening-hours block, and a legal row ([In Common With](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/f1844604-7696-4970-b30e-43a7669e9ee1)). Language in the bottom row ([VanMoof "NL | EN"](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/6c8e5010-32b5-41ce-b912-2b5e64332568); [Kalstore](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/b0f9e22e-2eb5-4f32-9bf4-8b04059d7207) adds payment badges).
- Optional oversized wordmark at the bottom (Graza footer, Kalstore), only when the logo is a wordmark.

## Anti-patterns (seen on Mobbin)
- Filler copy: "Welcome to our store", "Browse our latest products" over stock photos ([Shopify](https://mobbin.com/screens/453486d1-9819-4fac-8ded-4ee27b818c81)), and "OUR MISSION… a team of passionate people" ([Uvodo](https://mobbin.com/screens/b4184bb0-a34a-4898-af2c-36b7100952df)).
- "50+ / 12k+ / 100%" stat rows and floating badges ([v0](https://mobbin.com/screens/22b8fa46-01c6-4495-a64c-43be9c02c668) and [Replit](https://mobbin.com/screens/2a4a9113-02e6-43a4-a8c8-eb56cc293e48) AI-generated storefronts).
- Trust-badge triplets ("SSL Encrypted / Fast & Reliable Shipping / Verified by Visa") and "Powered by …" ([Uvodo checkout flow](https://mobbin.com/flows/b3dff2d7-797b-47a3-b7d5-66760ad0e801)).
- A faceted filter sidebar for a tiny catalogue ([bella](https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/a0e31a16-71e3-441f-a2c2-5c2cf593cbc2)).
- Painting everything in primary when primary is weak.

## Accessibility for config-driven colours
- `primaryContrast` on `primary` ≥ 4.5:1. Check at build time and fail, or auto-pick black/white.
- `text` and body-size `muted` ≥ 4.5:1 on both `background` and `surface`. Small grey-on-dark text (Assembly's dark hero) is where swaps break.
- Don't rely on `surface` vs `background` for card edges; add a hairline border from `text` at low alpha. Input and stepper borders ≥ 3:1.
- Two-tone focus ring, visible on background, surface and primary.
- Underline links; don't rely on primary hue.
- Scrim under text on photos.
- "Sold out" and errors as text or icons, not colour alone.
- Language switch as text ("FR | EN") with `lang`/`hreflang`, not flags.
- Theme Stripe's Payment Element via its Appearance API from the same tokens (`colorPrimary`, `colorBackground`, `colorText`, `colorDanger`, `fontFamily`), and run the same contrast checks on it.
